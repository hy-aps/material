# Algorithms for Problem Solving

This course focuses on designing algorithms using techniques that are usually not covered in basic algorithms courses and textbooks. The algorithms are implemented in the C++ programming language.

The course consists of an online course material and programming exercises. There are 80 exercises in total, and the course is graded as follows:

Solved exercises | Course grade
--- | ---
40--47 | 1
48--55 | 2
56--63 | 3
64--71 | 4
72--80 | 5

In addition, the course includes a supervised exam (graded pass/fail) based on the exercises you have solved during the course. Each student will get a personalized exam based on their submitted solutions.

When you have solved enough exercises for your target grade, contact the course organizer [by email](mailto:ahslaaks@cs.helsinki.fi). You will then receive instructions for the exam, which will be the final step of the course.
